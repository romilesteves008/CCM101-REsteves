# Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection

## Cloud Computing Mission Reflection

Throughout this mission, I learned more about cloud computing and the different platforms available for businesses. Among AWS, Azure, and GCP, **AWS impressed me the most** because of its wide range of services, flexibility, scalability, and strong support for different types of applications. I also learned that each platform has its own strengths and can be useful depending on the needs of a company.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three are major cloud service providers that offer services such as **compute, storage, databases, networking, security, and monitoring**. They allow businesses to deploy applications without needing to maintain all their physical hardware. They also support scalability, allowing resources to increase or decrease depending on demand. This makes cloud computing more flexible and cost-effective compared with maintaining traditional infrastructure.

I learned that **business requirements strongly influence cloud platform selection**. A company must consider its budget, application requirements, security needs, scalability, existing technology, location, and technical skills of its employees. The best cloud platform is not necessarily the one with the most features, but the one that best matches the organization's goals and requirements.

Acting as a **Cloud Solutions Consultant** taught me how important it is to understand a client's needs before recommending a solution. I learned to compare different cloud platforms, identify their advantages and disadvantages, and choose a solution based on practical business requirements rather than personal preference.

Finally, completing this mission improved my **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio**. I now have better documentation, reflections, comparisons, and evidence of my learning. My portfolio demonstrates not only my knowledge of cloud platforms but also my ability to analyze requirements and recommend appropriate cloud solutions. Overall, this mission helped me become more confident in understanding and applying cloud computing concepts.
